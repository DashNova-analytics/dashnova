## ✨ Overview

DashNova is a modern Business Intelligence platform designed to help organizations understand their data without requiring complex analytics tools.

Upload spreadsheets, monitor business performance, explore interactive dashboards, generate AI-driven insights, and forecast future trends—all from a clean, intuitive interface.

Built with a modern React ecosystem and enhanced by Google Gemini AI, DashNova enables businesses to make faster, smarter, and data-driven decisions.
