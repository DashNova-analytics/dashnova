# 🚀 DashNova

> AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform for Modern Businesses

DashNova is an intelligent Business Intelligence platform that transforms raw business data into actionable insights using AI, analytics, forecasting, and interactive dashboards.

Instead of simply displaying charts, DashNova helps businesses understand **what happened, why it happened, and what to do next.**
